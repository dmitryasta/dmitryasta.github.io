Automation is the second pillar of the offer for a practical reason. Entering a market creates a large amount of repetitive work: leads to answer, orders to plan, shipments to track, reports to assemble. When this work is done by hand, it is done late and incompletely.

My own experience is on the operating side. In 2026 I built a sales forecast and order-planning model so that the next delivery arrived before key products ran out, and kept analytics on stock, order history and assortment efficiency for a retail chain of ten stores. In an earlier role I introduced a CRM holding more than a thousand clients and cut tender preparation time by 40 percent. Automation grows from such work.

The service pairs naturally with [local representation](../local-commercial-representation/) and with the [market entry](../serbia-market-entry/) project itself.
