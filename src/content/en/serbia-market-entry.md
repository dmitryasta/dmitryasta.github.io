Serbia is a market where many entry projects stall for ordinary reasons: the price looks right at the factory and wrong on the shelf, the first distributor is chosen because he answered first, or nobody follows up after the samples are sent. A market entry project is therefore built around the chain, not around a single contact: **manufacturer, import, distribution, retail or B2B customer, repeat sales**.

I work on the ground in Belgrade, speak the language of Russian-speaking manufacturers and of Serbian counterparties, and use the same discipline I used when running a food import and distribution operation in 2026: documents first, landed cost first, and a weekly rhythm of follow-up.

The service can be taken as a whole or in parts.
