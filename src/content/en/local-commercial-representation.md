When I ran the Serbian side of a food import and distribution business in 2026, the most valuable routine was the simplest one: a weekly agreement of promotions with every supplier, a weekly delivery plan with the head office of the retail chain, and a stock sheet that was compared with the company system. Representation is built from such routines.

For a manufacturer abroad the effect is practical. Someone who knows the product and the language of both sides sits in Belgrade, sees the shelf, hears the buyer and writes it down every week. Decisions about price, assortment and promotions are then made on facts.

Representation often follows [distributor search](../find-distributors-in-serbia/) and the [first shipment](../import-to-serbia/), and can include [automation of reporting](../business-automation/).
