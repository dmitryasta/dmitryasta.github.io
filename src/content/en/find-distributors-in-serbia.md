Distributor choice is the decision with the longest consequences. A weak partner takes the product, places it in a few stores and stops answering, while the brand is considered closed for the market. A strong partner brings the first chain listing in the second or third month and reports every week.

My approach is to treat the search as a sales process: define the criteria, talk to candidates directly, compare them in one table and negotiate from a prepared price ladder. The price ladder comes from the [landed-cost calculation](../serbia-market-research/), so that nobody agrees to a margin that the shelf price cannot carry.

If you do not need a distributor but a person who represents your company on the spot, see [local commercial representation](../local-commercial-representation/).
