Most expensive mistakes in market entry are made before the first shipment: an unrealistic shelf price, a wrong channel, a missed certification step. A short assessment costs a fraction of one failed container.

The assessment is deliberately practical. I walk the shelves, collect real prices, talk to people in the trade, and then put the figures into a landed-cost model. The result is a document that a director can read in twenty minutes and use to make a decision.

If the answer is positive, the natural next steps are [distributor search](../find-distributors-in-serbia/) and the [first shipment](../import-to-serbia/). If it is negative, you have saved the cost of learning it in the market.
