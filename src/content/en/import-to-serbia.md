In 2026 I worked as the only on-site representative of a food import and distribution company in Belgrade. The role covered the full import cycle: production at the manufacturer, labelling, origin documents, transit through Europe, clearance in Serbia, receiving at the warehouse and weekly deliveries to stores, with transport by truck and by container through the port of Bar and two re-export shipments to Montenegro.

That experience is the basis of this service. It means I know which documents delay a shipment, how long each step really takes and where the cost is hidden. It does not replace the customs broker, who remains responsible for the declaration.

Import is rarely a separate question. It is connected with the [entry model](../serbia-market-entry/) and with the [distributor](../find-distributors-in-serbia/) who will take the goods after clearance.
