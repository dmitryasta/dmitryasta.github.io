Serbian producers often have a good product and a limited view of foreign markets: who buys, at what price, with what terms of payment and under what labelling rules. The service puts the commercial work in order before documents and trucks are involved.

My own practical experience is on the import side of the Russia, Serbia, Montenegro route, including the origin documents and the border and port logistics. The export service uses the same knowledge from the other end of the chain and the same rule: calculate the price at the buyer's door first.

Export can be combined with [import to Serbia](../import-to-serbia/) for companies that trade in both directions, and with [market research](../serbia-market-research/) for new product lines.
