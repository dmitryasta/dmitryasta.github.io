Most costly mistakes in export are made before the first shipment: a price that does not survive the route to the shelf, a channel that does not buy your category, a rule noticed after the label is printed. Research before shipping is meant to find these early. This article describes a method that can be carried out by an exporter or by a consultant in Belgrade. It also covers pricing for FMCG goods in Serbia and the calculation of the full landed cost of a delivery. Information is current as of 30 October 2026.

## 1. Write down the decision

State in one sentence what the research must decide, for example: "Should we produce a trial batch of 20 pallets for Serbia at an ex-works price of X?" A decision defines what to measure. Without it, research turns into a description of the market.

## 2. Collect shelf data

Visit stores in the channels you consider and record, for each comparable product:

- brand, product, pack size and unit price;
- regular and promotional shelf price, and the date;
- position on the shelf and number of facings;
- origin and the importer named on the label;
- claims on the pack (organic, traditional, no additives) and the language of the label.

The importer named on a label is a source of leads: it shows who already brings comparable goods to Serbia. Repeat the survey after a few weeks, because promotions change prices.

## 3. Map the channels

List the channels open to your category: international and domestic chains, discounters, wholesalers, specialised stores, HoReCa and online. For the chains, trade press gives scale: the ten largest players reported about EUR 5.0 billion of combined 2025 revenue (figures as reported, mixing net revenue, turnover and total revenue). Traditional channels exist alongside: green markets alone reported turnover of RSD 57.8 billion for 2025, up 4.3 percent at current prices on 2024. Details of the chains are in [selling to Serbian retail chains](../selling-to-serbian-retail-chains/).

## 4. Build the price ladder

Work from the shelf price backwards to your ex-works price, and then forwards to test it:

1. Shelf price including VAT.
2. Minus VAT (20 percent, or 10 percent for goods on the reduced-rate list; confirm the rate per tariff code).
3. Minus retailer margin, distributor margin and importer margin, as agreed in negotiation, since typical figures are not published.
4. Minus logistics, customs and handling costs.
5. Minus import duty, which depends on the tariff code and the origin.
6. Equals the price you can accept ex-works.

If the result is lower than your cost plus target margin, the product needs a different pack, a different channel or a different price position. Run the model at two exchange rates and two margin scenarios.

## 5. Read official statistics for what they are

The Statistical Office publishes retail turnover each month. For September 2026 it reports turnover up 13.6 percent at current prices and 8.8 percent at constant prices compared with September 2025, and for January to September 2026 up 9.4 percent at current prices and 7.4 percent at constant prices. These are indices for total retail trade (NACE division 47) and they do not separate food. An older release includes an index for food, beverages and tobacco. The population estimate for 2025 is 6,549,901. No official annual food-retail market size was found, and a number from a third-party report should be labelled with its source and date.

## 6. Check the rules that change the price or block the route

- **Duty and origin.** The tariff follows the EU Combined Nomenclature; check the rate for your code under the agreement that applies to your goods.
- **VAT rate.** Confirm per tariff code.
- **Veterinary and phytosanitary status.** Animal-origin products need a veterinary decision, and the producing establishment must meet Serbian conditions. See [importing food products into Serbia](../importing-food-products-into-serbia/).
- **Labelling.** Serbian language, importer details, and the 2026 amendments to the rulebook.

## 7. Test with the trade

Calls with importers, distributors and buyers answer what desk research cannot: whether the category is open, what price is credible, what support is expected. Take samples and a price list to the meetings. Record objections as data.

## 8. Write the recommendation

A useful report ends with one of three results: enter (with channel, first volume and price), adapt (what to change in pack, price or certification) or wait (and what would change the answer). It lists sources, marks estimates as estimates and names what was not verified. The [Serbia market research](../../services/serbia-market-research/) service follows this method.
